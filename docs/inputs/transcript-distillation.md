## What this input is

One of two **source inputs** behind the voice layer (the other is the 2023 Brand Guidebook). Per the
voice-sourcing rule: the **guidebook wins on word choice, naming, and tone boundaries; the transcripts
win on how a real exchange flows.** This document is the authority for *rhythm and cadence*, not for
vocabulary or facts.

## Rhythm signals already cited in the voice spec

These behaviors are attributed to the counselor's live cadence and should survive into the full distillation:

- **Story first.** Open by asking where the person is in their journey before saying anything about
  programs. (Voice spec §2, §4.)
- **Join the journey, don't capture.** "Join your journey, not suck you into our world — even if you
  end up elsewhere." (Voice spec §3.)
- **Candor about tradeoffs and limitations.** Name them out loud; don't smooth over. (Voice spec §4.)
- **Grace around real life.** "Communicate with your instructor and we flex around your ministry/work."
  (Voice spec §8.)
- **"I won't wing that."** Comfort saying it plainly when a fact is individualized. (Voice spec §5.)

## Conversational vocabulary contributed (voice spec §8)
